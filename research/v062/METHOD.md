# v062 method and acceptance scope

This is a fresh finite N8 continuation of the braid-2 U pair in two declared engines. It extends the retained v060 endpoint at ratio=.99100 to 1.00000. A separate numerical plan binds the exact runner, model sources, predecessor records, six seeds and six saved frame arrays before execution. No original source, record, failure or test result is replaced.

The sampled ratios are .99100, .99150, .99200, .99300, .99400, .99500, .99600, .99700, .99800, .99900 and 1.00000. The .991 state is a repeated join, not a new unique campaign state. Coarse checks occur at steps 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Thus the first comparison has two .0005 fine steps versus one .001 coarse step; every subsequent comparison has two .001 steps versus one .002 step. The endpoint receives a genuine coarse/fine comparison. The coarse frames retain the predecessor's orientation rather than being reset to the fine frames.

At the join, all six roots are refined from their exact predecessor coordinates and must move less than 1e-8. The two selected eigenspaces must overlap both prior temporal and coarse frames above 1−1e-7; the newly aligned temporal frame entries must differ from their predecessor by less than 1e-7. Frame hashes, checkpoint/aggregate equality, dimension 1060, reality, finiteness and orthonormality are required. The repeated spatial label must equal the predecessor's. Subsequent individual temporal charges must equal the inherited signs; the later spatial labels are measured, not forced to match historical values.

Root refinement uses bounded least squares in the explicit unwrapped chart [0,1] × [0,1.1], final optimizer success/status, residual and direct gap below 1e-6, and chart margin above .005. U2 is allowed above f2=1; no finite-cutoff Hamiltonian is implicitly wrapped. The maximum root jump is .035; same-index roots must remain separated by .003. Two U roots must be more than three loop radii apart. Only these six seeded roots are tracked; this is not an exhaustive node count over the chart.

Spatial comparison transports the U1 frame to U2 at 128 and 256 base steps. It includes projected adjacent-node seeds and log-spaced subdivisions. Both exterior gaps are sampled; every sampled interior local minimum is refined by a bounded scalar optimizer, with final status, bracket membership, finiteness, non-worsening value and gap above 1e-5 required. Endpoints remain included as sampled gap candidates. This is a located finite minimum, not a global or continuous lower bound. All transported overlaps must exceed .1, the spatial mesh determinant must exceed .99, and the absolute rectangle determinant must exceed .99. Coarse/fine parameter-frame determinants must exceed .99.

Each state measures temporal charges at both U nodes and the direct spatial B charge for radius .006 at 64/128 points and radius .003 at 128 points. Only the exact phase-resolution failure permits the declared 256/512/512 retry. Other errors reject immediately. Radius/mesh charges must agree; the spatial charge must equal the rectangle orientation times the temporal B charge. Temporal signs in different engine gauges are not equated. The original v060 singular crossing and its deliberate isolation rejection are retained evidence, not repeated in this extension.

Measurements write aggregate progress and immutable atomic frame checkpoints. A failure retains the completed prefix and error/optimizer attempts. Sources, thresholds and meshes are not revised while the numerical workers run. At most two workers run, each with one BLAS thread.

Reconciliation recomputes root displacements, stored-frame orthogonality, overlaps, orientation determinants and temporal/coarse polar-alignment conditions. It checks exact checkpoint/aggregate agreement and all NPZ hashes without redoing the full eigensolver or charge loops. The first join is compared directly to v060's arrays. Historical N4/N6 records are compared at ten common ratios. The new .9915 point has no matched historical measurement. The old v042 held-state linkage remains contextual through its runner and State source rather than an embedded raw-record protocol.

The runtime records differ from v060 only in the executable invocation name: python versus python3. Both paths resolve to the same binary and hash; every other recorded field is required to match. Both original records are retained. This executable check belongs to publication reconciliation, not an independently authenticated execution history.

The coverage ledger uses the retained v056 campaign family map plus named v058–v062 evidence. It does not upgrade checkpoint-only, event-only, gap-only or historical frame evidence to a whole-campaign N8 result. Scientific references, physical acceptance targets, microscopic tunnelling strain laws and infinite-cutoff conclusions remain separate open work.

Publication has a second freeze after measurements. It binds every current Python file, the reconciled results and source ledger, all preserved prior files and the complete test run. The report's test count comes from actual collected/passed cases and hashed JUnit/execution/stdout artifacts, not fixed prose. The deterministic full ZIP can be reconstructed from its supplied tree under the matching runtime; it does not require a missing prior ZIP.
