# S0y: retained spectral-window evidence to contour-contract adapter

Parent `1bcfe9f03f49c64407c6547eb7edd906646580a5`. Follows the completed S0x audit. Synthetic interface evidence only; physical S1–S4 and v078 remain outside this packet.

## Why this packet exists

S0x closed the exact-rational and positive-`rho` refusal gaps, but its
`self_adjoint`, `fixed_contour` and `uniform_over_parameter_domain` fields
were still declarations. Continuing to add transport variants would not close
that provenance boundary.

S0y instead fixes the smallest auditable transfer interface. It retains a
four-cell, two-dimensional **synthetic S1-shaped** spectral-window record and
derives one exact contour contract from the complete cell cover. It does not
run the physical model, reuse archived floating eigenvalues or declare a
physical spectral window.

## Exact synthetic fixture

The retained assembly artifact is the exact rational diagonal family

    H(x,y) = diag(-3-x, -1/3+x, 1/3-y, 3+y),
    (x,y) in [0,1/8] x [0,1/8].

It is split into four closed rational cells. Each cell records exact enclosures
for the selected indices `[1,2]`, the nearest lower and upper complement, the
self-adjoint assembly evidence ID and the derivative-contract ID. The checker
requires the four cell interiors to cover the required rectangle exactly once.
Gaps and overlaps are distinct refusals.

Across the complete cover the adapter derives

    cluster interval = [-1/3, 1/3],
    center c = 0,
    rho = 1/3,
    complement distance g = 3.

The fixed circular contour is not supplied by the fixture. The declared policy
chooses the exact max-margin midpoint

    r = (rho + g)/2 = 5/3,
    d = min(r-rho, g-r) = 4/3.

The uniform derivative evidence is `(H1,H2,H3)=(1,0,0)`. Applying the same
Riesz-contour derivative formulas reviewed in S0w/S0x yields

    (K,K1,K2) = (15/16, 45/32, 1485/256).

Every quantity is reconstructed with `fractions.Fraction`; no float or integer
truncation enters the adapter.

## What is derived, and what remains blocked

For this fixture, `fixed_contour` and `uniform_over_parameter_domain` become
derived consequences of:

1. one hash-bound exact self-adjoint assembly artifact;
2. a common selected pair and provenance across every cell;
3. an exact, non-overlapping cover of the required parameter rectangle;
4. locally ordered cluster/complement enclosures; and
5. a positive global separation `g > rho`.

The accepted result is nevertheless marked `DECLARED_UNEXECUTED` and
`eligible_for_physical_transport = false`. The adapter deliberately refuses an
input relabeled as physical S1 evidence. A physical successor must first retain
the actual exact assembly artifact, the accepted/unresolved S1 partition,
inertia shifts and signed pivots, per-cell spectral enclosures, derivative
evidence and a reviewed physical-evidence schema. This packet does not create
or simulate those records.

## Refusal coverage

Twenty-one executed mutations cover schema and scope, physical-evaluation
claims, assembly hashes, contour policy, invalid or out-of-domain cells, cover
gaps and overlaps, duplicate IDs, uncertified cells, assembly/self-adjoint/index
provenance, local spectral ordering, missing or invalid bounds, derivative
completeness and provenance, negative derivative bounds, and loss of global
uniform separation.

The independent verifier also reconstructs every retained cell enclosure
directly from the exact diagonal formula, recomputes the global window,
contour, resolvent margin and `(K,K1,K2)`, checks source/note bindings, and
exercises result, environment, source, evidence, completion-marker and
overwrite refusals.

## Evidence limits

S0y is an interface-and-refusal proof over one synthetic diagonal family. It
does not certify graphene assembly, a physical S1 partition, physical spectral
isolation, projector or transport execution, higher seam derivatives, the full
`eta/rho/L/L2/L3` composition, Q3/P3, a physical class, cutoff agreement,
continuous-path coverage, or any v078 correction. No numerical sweep is run.
