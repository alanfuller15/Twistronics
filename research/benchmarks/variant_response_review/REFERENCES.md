# Source notes

Primary sources checked 22 September 2026:

- [Tarnopolsky, Kruchkov and Vishwanath, arXiv:1808.05250](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05250): approximately 0.586 is a reported first magic coupling of the chiral continuum model. The response appropriately treats its 0.01° grid result as a sampled minimum, not an exact magic angle.
- [Ahn, Park and Yang, arXiv:1808.05375](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05375): the abstract and equation (19) use total winding −2e₂.
- [Published PRX PDF, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.9.021013](https://journals.aps.org/prx/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevX.9.021013): its abstract on printed page 021013-1 states +2e₂, but equation (19) on printed page 021013-6 and equation (31) on printed page 021013-7 give e₂ = −Nₜ/2.

The response's observation about the PRX abstract is supported, but “arXiv versus PRX convention split” is too broad: the published PDF itself contains the differing signs. Cite the specific equation and derive the mapping to the code's orientation convention. The review does not resolve that source inconsistency. Withdrawing the signed numerical claim is appropriate; the finite magnitude check remains the narrower statement.
