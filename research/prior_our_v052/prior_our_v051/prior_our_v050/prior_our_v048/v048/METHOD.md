# Executed measurement scope

The primary protocol was frozen before numerical execution. All primary source/anchor hashes are checked before each worker runs. Both cutoff_tol defaults are now explicit in the adapter; the uploaded engine sources are unchanged. The finite-tunneling patch is a separately tested optional replacement, not the source of primary results.

Each fold window uses two finite-difference Jacobian steps (2e-5, 1e-5), nondegeneracy checks at 2e-4 and 1e-4, and nine distinct-root continuation states. Charge is measured only at the first and near-fold states. The three loop trials are (r,256), (r,512), (r/2,512); a phase-resolution-only fallback uses (r,1024), (r,2048), (r/2,2048). Other failures stop the measurement. The comparison path uses 128/256 base meshes with both exterior-gap minima located and locally refined. The fold script's just-open sample uses grid18 and its far-open sample uses grids18/24. PLAN.json lists the available fold search meshes; it does not mean both were used at every fold-script sample.

The gapped-connection stage additionally checks the just-open sides of the upper, birth and final-annihilation windows on both 18/24 grids. Every one of its eight states per engine/cutoff checks all five relevant adjacent gaps with edge/corner and prior-basin seeds. It then checks five band/groups on two cycle meshes, two offsets and two torus directions. Selected-group isolation, polar-overlap, sewing overlap and sewing norm-loss thresholds are retained from the earlier harness.

The five samples between upper annihilation and flat birth include both open-side endpoints, the accepted ratio-1.04 bridge, and one midpoint on each side of that bridge. The three samples after final annihilation include its open side, a midpoint and A=−0.30. Parameter values depend on each independently located event root, so comparison is by the named leg and state rather than pretending every engine samples identical floating-point parameters.

Root identity between the flat birth's initial pair and final annihilation's initial pair is checked to 1e-6. Gapped bridge/endpoint minima must match the preserved same-engine/cutoff anchors within 1e-6 meV, and their cycle signs must agree exactly. These joins do not transport or compare unsaved absolute historical frame signs. They establish the recorded root/relative-label and gapped-checkpoint identities.

These are finite sampled numerical checks. They neither exclude an unsampled event between parameter points nor prove a global minimum over momentum. “Accepted” means the stated numerical gates passed. Two engines with a common estimator do not supply an independent test of that estimator or the physical model. The preparation root and lower-inventory probes test one N4 state each; they do not close the preparation route.
