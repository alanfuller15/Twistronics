# Bounded N8 lower unlink replay

Selection: v055 supplies complete accepted N4/N6 lower unlink records in both engines. Its event is distinct from v058's endpoint. The retained fold brackets, source and detailed gates were read before choosing this batch. HISTORICAL_TARGETS.json hashes the exact source records and retains their definitions, initial roots, fold diagnostics, nearest pre-event state, charge labels and opening gaps. No incoming partner script is imported.

NUMERICAL_PLAN.json freezes numerical runner/helper hashes and retained engine/measurement inputs before execution. It fixes T in [-0.4,0], A=0.2, B=-0.4, phi=0, ratio=0.8, theta=1.05, eps=0.003, constant tunnelling and lab_nn_full. The explicit fractional chart is [0,1] squared. BM linear and reference exact geometry and their cutoff tolerances stay distinct. N8 seeds and initial fold guesses come from each engine's retained N6 result, but acceptance does not force historical parameter equality.

Fold solver: converge the two projected-Hamiltonian components and a scaled Jacobian determinant in bounded momentum/parameter coordinates. Two spatial derivative steps, 2e-5 and 1e-5, must agree in T within 1e-6. Optimizer status, success, message and evaluations are attached to each returned trial. Accept only a gap/residual below 1e-6, rank-one Jacobian with singular values above 1 and below 1e-3, external gap above 1e-5, and chart margin above 0.005. The engine adapter's runtime reality/Hermiticity and eigen-residual/orthogonality checks remain active.

Nondegeneracy: two steps, 2e-4 and 1e-4, measure curvature along the spatial null direction and the transverse T slope in a local real frame. Require nonzero curvature/slope, agreement within 1%, and a prediction of roots on the T>fold side. The squared-separation coefficient is -8 times slope/curvature. The sign of the local frame can change both derivatives; that ratio is invariant under this sign choice.

Continuation: nine regular states from T=0 to fold+0.001, plus fold+0.004 and fold+0.00025, ordered toward the collision. Each state refines two distinct roots inside the chart with residual/gap below 1e-6, separation above 0.001 and individual step below 0.06. Three near-fold offsets test separation squared against the coefficient times offset within 2%. This is a finite local asymptotic check, not an interval theorem or infinite-cutoff fit.

Charge: T=0 and fold+0.001 have mesh/radius checks using the retained pair_measure and spatial helpers. Both comparison-path exterior gaps are located and checked, spatial transports at 128/256 steps must agree in orientation, and 256/512/512 loop samples compare two radii. Only unresolved phase increments allow the recorded 1024/2048/2048 retry. Both charges must be opposite unit windings. No charge is transported through the actual collision. The extra closest station is not assigned a charge measurement.

Opening: at fold-0.001 and fixed T=-0.4, use the v058 search with 18/24 chart intervals and 24/48 edge intervals, including both faces, all sampled minima, corner/inward seeds and the fold vicinity. Bounded L-BFGS-B, SLSQP and Nelder-Mead attempts are checked for success, finite/non-worsening/re-evaluated value and projected gradient. Selected minima must have positive finite-difference curvature at two steps; meshes and edge minima must agree within 0.001 meV. Positive finite searches do not certify a global root count.

The new continuation/solver and saved-evidence validator were read and assertion-tested. The retained engine construction and guarded measurement paths were reviewed in this and the preceding batch; historical files outside that scope were not fully reviewed. Existing imports do not call legacy Euler/braid certification entry points. No new dependency/CVE or whole-project security audit is claimed.

Every completed station is checkpointed; numerical rejection retains the partial event and available failure diagnostics. Source/runtime changes reject the case. Publication requires the complete event structure, revalidates stored diagnostics, and checks a source/test/input/runtime-bound actual test run. Hashes are internal consistency checks, not independent attestation of physical truth or original execution timing.
