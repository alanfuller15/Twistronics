a

Keep the ruler honest

1 nm = 10 Å. The reference lattice spacing a is 0.246 nm. Each dot marks a two-carbon lattice cell; it is not one atom. Dot radius is illustrative; dot positions and the ruler use the stated length scale.

L

Why the pattern grows

L = a / [2 sin(θ/2)]

If a were drawn as 1 mm, one moiré repeat would be 54.6 mm. A smaller twist makes L larger.

k

A different kind of space

Real space tells us where things are. Momentum space organizes wave-like electron states. A point on the next map is a wave-vector coordinate, not a place on the sheet.

Model assumptions & source

This is the ideal, unstrained geometric moiré period of two equal lattices. The outlined cell belongs to the continuum alignment pattern; generic twist angles need not make an exactly repeating atomistic crystal. This lesson does not calculate bands, simulate relaxation, or predict a material’s behavior. The research maps use fixed, strained model cases; changing this slider does not change them.

Background: Bistritzer & MacDonald, Moiré bands in twisted double-layer graphene (2011). The moiré length formula follows by subtracting the two rotated reciprocal lattices. Here a means the lattice constant, not the shorter carbon–carbon bond length.

x,y

Coordinates, not millimeters

k = xG₁ + yG₂. x and y are dimensionless weights on reciprocal vectors. The square chart does not imply that physical momentum axes are perpendicular. We do not convert these values into real-space distance.

u,v

A local ruler

Inside the parent patch, u = 512x − 351 and v = 512y − 368. One local unit is 1/512 of a global fractional coordinate. A 512× linear zoom enlarges chart area by 262,144×.

ΔE

A gap is an energy difference

1 meV = 0.001 eV. The smallest retained gap is about 0.00309 meV, or 3.09 μeV. This is the difference E₉₉ − E₉₈ at one point. Marker size is fixed and does not represent gap size or certified area.

Sampling is not coverage. Refinement targeted four coarse cells. These 128 points are not a uniform validation set, and a small positive sampled gap does not bound the gap between points or prove a gap closure.
